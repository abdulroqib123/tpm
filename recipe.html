Savory French Toast Sticks

Savory French Toast Sticks made with bread, eggs, milk and grated cheese. It has a salty taste and is soft and delicous. Perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • Four slices of bread
  • Two eggs
  • Quater cup milk
  • One tbsp grated cheese
  • Salt, black pepper and chili flakes

Preparation

  1. Cut bread into sticks like rectangualar cubes.
  2. Whisk eggs with milk, cheese, salt, pepper, and chili.
  3. Dip sticks and fry until golden on all side.
  4. Serve warm with grated cheese sprickled on it.
Note: Do not dip the bread in the egg for too long so it doent get too suggy

Cooking term

Blanch

Whisk

Broil
